The header uses [dust-wars-mark.png](../src/assets/dust-wars-mark.png), a

## Generation prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: production logo emblem for the browser trading game Dust Wars, displayed at 52px in a near-black application header.

Generate ONE original premium emblem on a truly transparent background. Square composition with the emblem occupying 86% of the canvas, centered, clean silhouette, no surrounding presentation. A distinctive interlocking D and W monogram inspired by urban stencil lettering and an angular street insignia: bold confident geometric strokes, compact shield-like outer silhouette with subtly broken edges that suggest dust erosion. The D should be readable on the left and W integrated into the lower/right structure, with deliberate negative space. Restrained warm antique gold #d4a860 with darker bronze #b8893f edge accents, flat graphic finish with only very subtle patina. High-quality professional game identity, sharp edges, thick strokes and uncluttered construction that remains readable at 40–56 pixels. Not a generic outlined diamond and not an ornate heraldic crest.

Transparent exterior AND negative space holes. No background fill, no black square, no cast shadow, no glow, no neon, no 3D mockup, no photographic scene, no drugs, no weapons, no characters, no crown, no coins. No full text or tagline; only the D and W forming the emblem. Deliver the final finished isolated emblem as a PNG with genuine alpha transparency.
